Hi, my name is Filipe Azevedo.
<p>I was born in a small town in the North of Portugal called Vila do Conde. I received the diploma in 1999, the M.Sc. in 2003, both in Electrical and Computer Engineering from <a href="https://sigarra.up.pt/feup/pt/web_page.inicial">Faculty of Engineering of the University of Porto (FEUP)</a>, Porto, Portugal, and the Ph.D. degree in 2008 on Electrical and Computer Engineering from <a href="https://www.utad.pt/">University of Trás-os-Montes e Alto Douro (UTAD)</a>, Vila Real, Portugal. </p>
<p>Currently I am an Associate Professor of Power Systems at the <a href="https://isep.pt/">School of Engineering, Polytechnic Institute of Porto (ISEP/IPP)</a> in Porto, Portugal.</p>
<p>My main research interests are Economics applied to Energy Markets, Decision-Support System techniques applied to Economics Engineering problems, Artificial Intelligence (A.I.) applications, Smart Grids, and Renewable Energy production integration on power systems. I have also interests in National Security related problems, encryption and cryptography.</p>
<p>I have recently published a book titled "MATLAB: From Fundamentals to Artificial Intelligence". The paper edition is available in Portuguese at <a href="https://www.booki.pt/loja/prod/matlab-do-fundamental-a-intelig-ncia-artificial/9789899177857/">Booki</a>, <a href="https://www.bertrand.pt/livro/matlab-do-fundamental-a-inteligencia-artificial-filipe-azevedo/32344497">Bertrand</a>, and <a href="https://www.fnac.pt/MATLAB-Do-Fundamental-a-Inteligencia-Artificial-Filipe-Azevedo/a13193007">FNAC</a>. The eBook version is available in both <a href="https://www.booki.pt/loja/prod/matlab-do-fundamental-a-intelig-ncia-artificial-ebook/9789899177864/">Portuguese</a> and <a href="https://www.booki.pt/loja/prod/matlab-from-fundamentals-to-artificial-intelligence-ebook/9789899177871/">English</a>.</p>
